We gave our trailer a sense of balance this week. All open source.

This was a big week for TrailCurrent. Here's what shipped across our open-source vehicle intelligence platform:

Know exactly how level you are. Our new Plateau module uses a 9-axis IMU to measure pitch and roll in real time, calculates per-corner height adjustments, and sends it all over CAN bus to your dashboard. No more guessing with a bubble level. We designed the PCB, milled it on a desktop CNC, and had it running on a real trailer in under a week.

"Hey Peregrine, how's my level?" Our on-vehicle voice assistant now runs a 1B-parameter LLM on a Qualcomm NPU with zero cloud dependency. We swapped runtimes (Ollama to Genie), cut false wake word triggers, and added voice commands for leveling data and GPS. Everything processes locally at ~12 tokens per second.

Full-stack updates, top to bottom. Our edge gateway (Headwaters) got new Node-RED workflows and cloud config sync. Our web dashboard (Farwatch) picked up leveling controls and better device management. Our environmental sensor (Borealis) got a new sensor, a revised PCB, and a redesigned enclosure. We expanded our KiCAD component libraries with 10+ new parts.

Every line of code, every schematic, every 3D model is open source. No subscriptions. No vendor lock-in. Your vehicle, your data.

We're building the platform we wished existed when we started outfitting our own trailer. If open-source vehicle intelligence sounds interesting to you, follow along or jump in.
